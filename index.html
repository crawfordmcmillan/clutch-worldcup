The Hidden Costs That Will Shock You

Based on comprehensive research of actual international visitor experiences

💸 Mandatory Tipping

+$350-500

18-22% on EVERYTHING - restaurants, bars, taxis, hotels. Not optional in the US like it is in Europe, Asia, or Australia. Servers earn $2.13/hour and depend on your tips.

🏨 Resort Fees

+$33/night

NOT included in advertised hotel prices. Covers WiFi, pool, gym you may never use. Subject to additional taxes. A $200/night hotel actually costs $240+ per night.

🏥 Medical Costs

$1,000-3,000

One ER visit without insurance. No universal healthcare for tourists. Medical insurance ($200-400) is MANDATORY - hospital stays can cost $50,000-200,000+.

💳 Sales Tax Surprise

+8-10%

NOT included in displayed prices (unlike VAT). Added at checkout. $100 item costs $108. Every purchase, every meal, every hotel bill.

✈️ Baggage Fees

+$200-400

Family of 4 roundtrip. $30-75 per checked bag. Overweight fees $50-200 more. US airlines charge for everything.

⚖️ Legal Traps

FELONY risk

Cannabis legal in California, 180 DAYS JAIL in Texas. Open carry legal in Missouri, BANNED in Florida. What's legal in one state is a crime in another.
