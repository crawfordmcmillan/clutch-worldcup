Problem Statement

The Core Problem

International visitors to the 2026 World Cup arrive with expectations shaped by their home countries' systems:

  • VAT refunds (Europe, Australia, Canada)
  • Comprehensive public transit (Europe, Asia)
  • Included pricing (VAT systems show final prices)
  • Universal healthcare coverage
  • Minimal/optional tipping (5-10% vs 18-22% mandatory)
  • Different legal frameworks (cannabis, firearms, alcohol)

Reality: The US operates fundamentally differently

  1. Financial shock: 25-35% budget overruns from hidden costs
  2. Legal risk: What's legal in one state is a felony in another
  3. Logistical failure: Underestimated distances, car dependency
  4. Cultural confusion: Tipping, personal space, punctuality expectations
  5. Missed savings: Don't know about CityPASS, OMNY, free museums

Specific Pain Points

Financial Surprises

  • Tipping adds $350-500 per family (18-22% mandatory)
  • Resort fees average $33/night (not in advertised prices)
  • Sales tax 0-10% added at checkout (not displayed)
  • Medical insurance required ($200-400) - ER visits cost $1,000-3,000+
  • Foreign transaction fees: 2-3% on all purchases
  • Total impact: $7,500 budget becomes $9,965 (33% more)

Legal Traps

  • Cannabis: Legal in CA/WA/NY/MA, FELONY in TX/GA (180 days jail for <2oz)
  • Firearms: Open carry legal in TX/MO, banned in CA/FL/NY
  • Alcohol: Sales hours vary wildly, dry counties exist
  • Federal property: Cannabis always illegal (parks, federal buildings)

Logistical Disasters

  • NYC to LA: 2,800 miles (41 hours driving) - visitors try in one week
  • Gillette Stadium: 28 miles from Boston, closer to Providence
  • No public transit outside 5-6 major cities - car rental necessary
  • Food portions 42% larger than European - one meal = 2-3 servings

Cultural Confusion

  • "On time" = 5-10 minutes early (lateness = disrespect)
  • Personal space: 2 feet minimum distance
  • "How are you?" = greeting, not real question
  • Bathroom stalls have huge gaps (privacy shock)
