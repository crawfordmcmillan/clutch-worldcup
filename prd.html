THE PROBLEM

Americans don't explain how their pricing system works because they can't imagine not knowing it.

International visitors to the 2026 World Cup arrive with expectations shaped by their home countries' systems: VAT refunds, comprehensive public transit, included pricing, universal healthcare, minimal/optional tipping, and different legal frameworks.

The American Reality: A pricing system designed to show lower advertised costs while extracting maximum dollars through hidden fees, mandatory tipping, and taxes added at checkout.

Financial Shock

25-35% budget overruns from hidden costs. Hotels advertised at "$200/night" actually cost $701 after resort fees, taxes, and parking.

Legal Risk

What's legal in one state is a felony in another. Cannabis legal in Massachusetts = 180 days jail in Texas.

Logistical Failure

Underestimated distances and car dependency. "Boston's" stadium is 28 miles away in Foxborough.

THE SOLUTION

Clutch is Financial Self-Defense. We expose the pricing game, quantify every hidden cost, and provide geographic arbitrage strategies that save $1,000-2,500.

  • Exposes the pricing game (shows "$200/night" actually costs $701)
  • Quantifies every hidden cost ($2,465 in preventable spending)
  • Geographic arbitrage strategies (Providence vs Boston saves $1,500)
  • Legal risk mitigation (cannabis legal in MA = felony in TX)
  • World Cup-specific intelligence (match-day 440% surge pricing)
  • ROI: $99 to save $2,465 (pays for itself 25x over)
