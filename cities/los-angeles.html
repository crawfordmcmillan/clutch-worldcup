🎬 Los Angeles, California

Complete World Cup Visitor's Guide

🚗 CRITICAL: LA Traffic is NO JOKE

Locals recommend leaving 3+ HOURS before kickoff for match-day traffic.

Los Angeles has some of the worst traffic in the United States. During major events like World Cup matches, expect gridlock. A drive that's "20 minutes" on Google Maps will take 90+ minutes on match day. Plan accordingly or you WILL miss kickoff.

💸 Hidden Costs Specific to LA

1. Hotel Costs (The Reality)

Item Cost
Advertised room rate $200/night
Resort fee (common in LA) +$30-45/night
Hotel tax (~12-14%) +$28-32/night
Parking (ALWAYS separate in LA) +$35-60/night
ACTUAL TOTAL $293-337/night (47-68% more)

⚠️ LA Parking Costs Are Brutal

Unlike most US cities, LA hotels charge $35-60/night for parking on top of room rates and resort fees. Valet parking can be $60-80/night. This is NOT included in advertised prices. Budget an extra $210-420 for parking alone on a 7-night stay.

2. Transportation Nightmare

LA has virtually NO useful public transit. You NEED a car or will pay massive Uber costs.

Option Cost Feasibility
Public Transit (Metro) $1.75 per ride ❌ Doesn't go most places tourists need
Uber/Lyft (normal) $25-50 for cross-city trips ⚠️ Surge pricing 2-3X on match days
Uber to SoFi (match day) $80-150 ONE WAY ⚠️ $300-400 round trip for family
Rental Car $60-100/day + $50/day gas ✅ NECESSARY for LA
SoFi Stadium Parking $75-175 per game ✅ Must buy in advance
💡 LA Transportation Strategy:
  1. Rent a car ($60-100/day is cheaper than daily Uber)
  2. Book hotel with FREE parking (suburbs, not downtown)
  3. Buy SoFi Stadium parking pass in advance
  4. Leave 3+ HOURS before kickoff (traffic is real)
  5. Budget $400-600 total for week of transportation

3. Food & Tipping (Same 25-30% Markup)

  • Sales tax: 9.5% on all food (one of highest in US)
  • Tipping: 18-22% mandatory (servers earn $15-16/hr minimum wage in CA, but tips still expected)
  • Total markup: Menu price + 27-32% for tax + tip
  • Example: $25 meal → $32-33 actual cost

⚖️ California State Laws

🚫 Open Carry of Firearms = BANNED

California prohibits open carry of handguns statewide. This is a CRIMINAL OFFENSE. If you're from Texas, Missouri, or other states where open carry is legal, understand this is ILLEGAL in California. Even unloaded handguns cannot be openly carried.

✅ Cannabis is LEGAL (With Restrictions)

  • Adults 21+ can possess up to 1 oz
  • Licensed dispensaries throughout LA
  • Use ONLY in private spaces (not public, not hotels usually)
  • ⚠️ ILLEGAL on all federal property (national parks, federal buildings)
  • ⚠️ DO NOT take across state lines (federal felony)
  • ⚠️ DO NOT take to Texas/Georgia/Missouri (180 days jail there)

Other California Laws

  • Speed limits: 65 mph urban highways, 70 mph rural (strictly enforced)
  • DUI: 0.08% BAC, serious penalties
  • Smoking: Banned in all enclosed public places, some cities ban sidewalk smoking
  • Alcohol: 21+ only, relatively liberal hours compared to East Coast
  • Right on red: Allowed after full stop (unless posted)

💰 Money-Saving Strategies for LA

1. LA CityPASS - Save $100-160 Per Person

Best attraction value in LA

  • Cost: ~$130-150 adult
  • Includes: Universal Studios OR Warner Bros + 3 more attractions
  • Individual cost: $260-310 if bought separately
  • Savings: $109-164 per person (42% off)
  • For family of 4: Save $400-650 total

2. Free LA Attractions

  • Getty Center: FREE admission (world-class art museum, $20 parking)
  • Griffith Observatory: FREE, amazing views of Hollywood sign + city
  • Venice Beach Boardwalk: FREE, iconic LA experience
  • Santa Monica Pier: FREE to walk around (rides cost extra)
  • Hollywood Walk of Fame: FREE (just a sidewalk with stars)
  • The Broad Museum: FREE contemporary art (advance reservation required)

3. Budget Hotels Outside LA

Instead of $250-400/night downtown LA hotels:

  • Inglewood (near SoFi): $100-150/night, 10 min to stadium
  • El Segundo (near airport): $90-140/night, 20 min to stadium
  • Torrance/Carson: $80-120/night, 20-30 min to stadium
  • Savings: $100-200/night = $700-1,400 for week
  • Bonus: Often include free parking ($35-60/night value)

4. Food Strategies

  • Food trucks: $8-15 per meal (authentic, cheaper than restaurants)
  • In-N-Out Burger: $10-12 meal (CA institution, excellent value)
  • Korean BBQ (Koreatown): $20-30 all-you-can-eat (huge portions)
  • Mexican food (authentic): $8-15 per meal (better than touristy Mexican)
  • vs. Sit-down restaurants: $35-50 per person after tax + tip

🚗 Getting Around LA (Survival Guide)

Public Transit Reality Check

LA Metro exists but is almost useless for tourists.

  • Doesn't connect most tourist destinations
  • Doesn't go to beaches, Hollywood Hills, many neighborhoods
  • Slow (1-2 hour trips vs 20-30 min driving)
  • Limited hours
  • Bottom line: You NEED a car in LA

Rental Car is MANDATORY

Budget for car rental:

  • Rental: $50-100/day (compact to mid-size)
  • Insurance: $15-30/day (check if credit card covers)
  • Gas: $5-6/gallon (expensive in CA)
  • Parking: $10-25 at attractions, $35-60/night at hotels
  • Total: $100-200/day for car + gas + parking

The LA Traffic Survival Guide

  1. Leave 3x Google Maps Time: If it says 30 min, plan 90 min
  2. Avoid Rush Hours: 7-10am and 3-7pm are gridlock
  3. Match Day = Worse: World Cup traffic will be extreme
  4. Use Waze App: Real-time traffic routing (better than Google)
  5. Plan Nothing After Match: Leaving SoFi takes 60-90 min

🏨 Where to Stay

Near SoFi Stadium (Best for Matches)

Inglewood, El Segundo, Manhattan Beach area:

  • 10-20 minutes to stadium (vs 60-90 from downtown)
  • $100-180/night (vs $250-400 downtown)
  • Free parking common
  • Near LAX airport (convenient arrival/departure)
  • 30-45 min drive to Hollywood/Santa Monica

Downtown LA (Central but Expensive)

  • $250-400/night + $35-60 parking
  • 60-90 min to SoFi Stadium (traffic)
  • Central to attractions
  • Less safe at night than tourist areas

Santa Monica/Venice (Beach Areas)

  • $200-350/night
  • 45-90 min to SoFi (traffic dependent)
  • Safer, more touristy, expensive
  • Great if prioritizing beach experience
💡 Recommended Strategy: Stay near SoFi Stadium (Inglewood/El Segundo) for match days. Day-trip to Hollywood, beaches, downtown when NOT attending matches. Save $700-1,400 on hotels plus $200-400 on transportation.

🌡️ Weather in June/July

  • Temperature: 70-85°F (21-29°C) coastal, 85-95°F (29-35°C) inland
  • Humidity: Low (dry heat, easier than East Coast)
  • Rain: Almost zero (June/July is bone dry)
  • Marine layer: Coastal fog in mornings ("June Gloom")
  • What to pack: Light clothes, sunscreen (sun is intense), sunglasses
