⚽ Stadium & World Cup Information

Arrowhead Stadium

  • Full Name: GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium
  • Location: 1 Arrowhead Dr, Kansas City, MO 64129
  • Capacity: 76,416 (home of NFL's Kansas City Chiefs, largest in Missouri)
  • Surface: Platinum TE Paspalum natural grass
  • Opened: 1972 (renovated 2010)
  • Distance from Downtown KC: 8 miles (13 km) east - 15-20 minutes drive
  • Notable: Guinness World Record for loudest stadium (142.2 decibels), iconic architecture, hosted 2026 World Cup qualifying matches
  • Unique: Part of the Truman Sports Complex with Kauffman Stadium next door

⚖️ Missouri State Laws

❌ Cannabis is ILLEGAL

Missouri has NOT legalized recreational cannabis.

  • Medical marijuana legal (with MO medical card)
  • Recreational possession = criminal offense
  • ⚠️ DO NOT bring from legal states (CA, WA, NY, MA)
  • Crossing state line with cannabis = FEDERAL FELONY

✅ Open Carry is LEGAL (Constitutional Carry)

Missouri permits constitutional carry of firearms without permits for adults 21+. Some cities restrict this. Open carry is more common here than in coastal states.

Missouri-Specific Laws

  • Alcohol: Relatively liberal (Missouri has loose alcohol laws)
  • DUI: 0.08% BAC
  • Right on red: Allowed after full stop
  • Smoking: Comprehensive bans in public places

1. KC is ALREADY Cheap

Kansas City is the most affordable World Cup city. Even without special strategies, you'll save $1,000-2,000 vs coastal cities.

3. Free KC Attractions

  • National WWI Museum: $18 admission (worth it)
  • Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art: FREE admission
  • Country Club Plaza: Free to walk around

4. Transportation - Car Rental NEEDED

No useful public transit in Kansas City. You MUST rent a car.

  • Rental: $40-70/day (cheaper than coastal cities)
  • Gas: $3-3.50/gallon (cheaper than CA/FL)
  • Parking: Usually FREE (huge advantage)
  • Stadium parking: $75-175 per game (buy in advance)
