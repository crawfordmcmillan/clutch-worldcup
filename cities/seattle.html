⚽ Stadium & World Cup Information

Lumen Field

  • Full Name: Lumen Field (formerly CenturyLink Field, Qwest Field)
  • Location: 800 Occidental Ave S, Seattle, WA 98134 (Downtown Seattle)
  • Capacity: 68,740 for soccer (home of NFL's Seattle Seahawks, MLS's Seattle Sounders)
  • Surface: FieldTurf artificial surface
  • Opened: 2002
  • Distance from Downtown: In downtown Seattle - walkable from many hotels!
  • Notable: Known as one of the loudest stadiums in the world, hosted 2016 Copa América matches, MLS Cup Finals, international friendlies. Seattle Sounders hold MLS attendance records here.
  • Unique Feature: Partial roof amplifies crowd noise to record-breaking levels

✅ Seattle Advantage: No Car Needed!

Lumen Field is walkable from downtown hotels. You can SKIP the rental car and parking costs entirely. Use public transit, walk, or occasional Ubers. This saves $200-400 compared to car-dependent cities.

2. Tipping (18-22% Mandatory)

Seattle has HIGHEST minimum wage in US ($19.97/hour in 2026) but tipping is STILL expected at same 18-22% rate.

  • Restaurants: 18-22% of pre-tax bill
  • Coffee shops (very common): $1-2 per drink
  • Bars: $1-2 per drink or 20% of tab
  • Rideshare: 15-20%

⚖️ Washington State Laws

✅ Cannabis LEGAL (Since 2012)

  • Adults 21+ can possess up to 1 oz useable cannabis
  • 16 oz cannabis-infused solid products
  • 72 oz liquid cannabis products
  • 7g cannabis concentrate
  • Licensed dispensaries throughout Seattle
  • ⚠️ ILLEGAL on federal property (national parks)

✅ Open Carry Legal (But Unusual in Seattle)

Washington permits constitutional carry for firearms (21+). However, open carry is RARE in Seattle compared to rural Washington or other states. Concealed carry more common.

Washington-Specific Tax Law

Sales Tax Exemption for Non-Residents: Washington offers point-of-sale exemption for non-residents purchasing items for use outside Washington. This applies to retail purchases ONLY (not hotels, not restaurants).

💰 Money-Saving Opportunities

1. Seattle CityPASS - Save 47%

  • Cost: ~$119 adult
  • Includes: Space Needle, Seattle Aquarium, Argosy Cruises, + 2 more
  • Individual cost: $225 if bought separately
  • Savings: $106 per person (47%)

2. Free Seattle Attractions

  • Pike Place Market: Free to walk around, iconic Seattle experience
  • Kerry Park: Free, best view of Seattle skyline
  • Fremont Troll: Free, quirky Seattle landmark
  • Alki Beach: Free, waterfront views
  • Olympic Sculpture Park: Free outdoor art

3. Public Transit Actually Works Here!

Unlike LA or Miami, Seattle has USABLE public transit

  • Link Light Rail: $3.25 one-way, connects airport to downtown to stadium
  • ORCA Card: Unlimited day pass available
  • Buses: Comprehensive coverage downtown
  • Savings vs rental car: $50-100/day
  • Stadium is walkable from downtown hotels
💡 Seattle Transportation Strategy: DON'T rent a car. Use public transit ($3-6/day) vs rental car + parking ($100-150/day). Seattle is one of only 5-6 US cities where this actually works.

🌧️ Weather in June/July

Surprise: Seattle is DRY in Summer

Contrary to reputation, Seattle summers are BEAUTIFUL:

  • Temperature: 65-75°F (18-24°C) - perfect
  • Rain: Almost NONE in June/July (driest months)
  • Humidity: Low, comfortable
  • Daylight: 16+ hours (sun doesn't set until 9pm)
