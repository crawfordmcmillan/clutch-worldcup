🚫 Hard Rock Stadium is NOT in Miami

Hard Rock Stadium is in Miami Gardens - 15 miles (24 km) north of Miami Beach. It's 30-45 min drive without traffic, 60-90 min on match days. Don't book South Beach hotels assuming quick stadium access.

💸 Hidden Costs (Miami-Specific)

1. Miami Beach Resort Tax Nightmare

Item Cost
Advertised room rate $250/night
Resort fee (almost UNIVERSAL in Miami Beach) +$35-50/night
County tax (6%) + Convention tax (3%) +$22.50
Miami Beach city tax (4%) +$10
Parking (separate ALWAYS) +$35-55/night
ACTUAL TOTAL $352-397/night (41-59% more!)

⚠️ Miami Has HIGHEST Hidden Hotel Costs in US

Miami Beach hotels charge some of the highest resort fees ($35-50/night) PLUS city tax (4%) PLUS parking ($35-55/night) on top of already-expensive room rates. A "$200" hotel actually costs $300+.

2. Food & Drink (Tourist Trap Pricing)

  • South Beach restaurants: $25-45 per person BEFORE tax + tip
  • Sales tax: 7%
  • Tip: 18-22% (some add automatic 20%)
  • Total markup: 25-30% on menu prices
  • Example: $30 meal → $38-39 actual cost
  • Alcohol: $12-18 cocktails (before tax + tip = $15-23)

⚖️ Florida State Laws

❌ Cannabis is ILLEGAL for Recreational Use

Florida has NOT legalized recreational cannabis. Possession without a medical card is a criminal offense.

  • Medical marijuana legal (with Florida medical card only)
  • International visitors CANNOT get medical cards
  • Recreational possession = misdemeanor (jail + fines)
  • ⚠️ DO NOT bring from legal states (CA, NY, etc.)

❌ Open Carry BANNED in Florida

Unlike Texas or Missouri, Florida bans open carry of firearms in general public spaces. Only permitted during specific outdoor recreation (hunting, fishing, camping).

Florida-Specific Laws

  • Alcohol: Sales hours relatively liberal, but varies by county
  • Smoking: Partial bans (bars can permit smoking)
  • DUI: 0.08% BAC, strict enforcement
  • Right on red: Allowed after full stop

💰 Money-Saving Strategies

1. Stay AWAY from South Beach

💡 Best Money-Saving Strategy:

  • South Beach hotels: $300-500/night after fees
  • Miami Gardens (near stadium): $100-160/night
  • Fort Lauderdale (20 min north): $90-140/night
  • Savings: $200-360/night = $1,400-2,520 per week!

2. Free Miami Attractions

  • South Beach / Ocean Drive: Free (just a beach + street)
  • Art Deco Historic District: Free to walk around
  • Wynwood Walls: Free outdoor street art
  • Vizcaya Museum free day: Check schedule
  • Bayside Marketplace: Free (shops/restaurants cost money)

3. Miami CityPASS Alternative

Miami doesn't have CityPASS. Instead:

  • Go City Miami: Similar concept, 50% savings
  • Individual attractions: Often overpriced
  • Free beaches: Best Miami experience costs nothing

4. Food Strategies

  • Avoid South Beach restaurants: Tourist traps, expensive
  • Little Havana: Authentic Cuban food $8-15 per meal
  • Food trucks: $8-12 per meal
  • Grocery stores: Publix deli $6-10 meals
  • vs. South Beach: $35-50 per person after tax + tip

🚗 Transportation

Public Transit is TERRIBLE in Miami

Miami has almost no useful public transit. You NEED a car or will spend $50-100/day on Uber.

  • Metrorail: Limited routes, doesn't go to beaches or stadium
  • Buses: Slow, infrequent, unsafe reputation
  • Metromover: Free but ONLY covers small downtown loop
  • Bottom line: Rent a car

Rental Car Costs

  • Rental: $50-90/day
  • Insurance: $15-30/day
  • Gas: $3.50-4/gallon
  • Hotel parking: $35-55/night in Miami Beach
  • Stadium parking: $75-175 per game

🌡️ Weather in June/July

⚠️ Extreme Heat & Humidity Warning

  • Temperature: 85-95°F (29-35°C)
  • Humidity: 70-90% (feels like 105°F+)
  • Daily thunderstorms: Almost guaranteed 3-5pm
  • UV index: Extreme (sunburn in 15 minutes)
  • What to pack: Light clothes, STRONG sunscreen, hat, water bottle

This is the HOTTEST, most humid climate of all World Cup cities. Europeans/Australians often underestimate Miami summer heat.

🛡️ Safety

Generally Safe Tourist Areas

  • South Beach (touristy, safe during day/evening)
  • Wynwood (daytime, art district)
  • Coral Gables (wealthy, safe)
  • Coconut Grove

Be Cautious / Avoid

  • Liberty City (high crime)
  • Overtown (high crime)
  • Little Haiti at night
  • Anywhere isolated at night
