🚫 Levi's Stadium is NOT in San Francisco

Levi's Stadium is in Santa Clara - 45 miles (72 km) south of San Francisco. It's a 1-hour drive without traffic, 90+ minutes with traffic. Public transit exists but takes 90+ minutes. Don't book SF hotels assuming quick stadium access.

⚠️ San Francisco = Second Most Expensive World Cup City

Only NYC is more expensive. SF has astronomical hotel rates + parking costs ($40-70/night) + high taxes. Budget accordingly.

2. Food & Drink (Premium Pricing)

  • Restaurants: $25-50 per person BEFORE tax + tip
  • Sales tax: 8.5%
  • Tip: 20-22% (SF tipping culture is aggressive)
  • Total markup: 28-30% on menu prices
  • Example: $30 meal → $39-40 actual cost
  • Coffee: $5-7 (vs $2-3 in midwest)

⚖️ California State Laws

✅ Cannabis LEGAL (Since 2016)

  • Adults 21+ can possess up to 1 oz cannabis
  • 8g cannabis concentrate permitted
  • Licensed dispensaries throughout Bay Area
  • ⚠️ ILLEGAL on federal property (Golden Gate National Recreation Area)
  • ⚠️ ILLEGAL to smoke in public spaces (tickets issued)

❌ Open Carry BANNED in California

California has strictest gun laws in US. Open carry is banned statewide. Concealed carry requires permit (nearly impossible for non-residents to obtain).

California-Specific Laws

  • Plastic bags: Banned - bring reusable or pay $0.10 per bag
  • Straws: Only provided upon request (banned by default)
  • Alcohol: Sales 6am-2am daily
  • DUI: 0.08% BAC, strict enforcement
  • Right on red: Allowed after full stop (unless posted)
  • Smoking: Banned within 20 feet of public buildings

💰 Money-Saving Strategies

1. Stay in San Jose Instead of SF

💡 Best Money-Saving Strategy:

  • San Francisco hotels: $300-500/night after fees
  • San Jose (near stadium): $120-200/night
  • Fremont/Milpitas: $100-160/night
  • Savings: $180-340/night = $1,260-2,380 per week!

Why? Levi's Stadium is 45 miles from SF but only 5-10 miles from San Jose. You'll be closer to the stadium AND save massively on hotels.

2. Free Bay Area Attractions

  • Golden Gate Bridge: Free to walk/bike across
  • Golden Gate Park: Free (museums inside cost money)
  • Fisherman's Wharf: Free to walk around
  • Painted Ladies (Alamo Square): Free iconic photo spot
  • Cable Car Museum: Free
  • Lombard Street: Free to walk down "crookedest street"

3. San Francisco CityPASS - Save 45%

  • Cost: ~$89 adult
  • Includes: California Academy of Sciences, Blue & Gold Fleet Bay Cruise, Aquarium of the Bay + choice of Exploratorium OR de Young Museum
  • Individual cost: $160+ if bought separately
  • Savings: $71+ per person (45%)

4. Food Strategies

  • Avoid Fisherman's Wharf restaurants: Tourist traps, overpriced
  • Mission District: Authentic tacos/burritos $8-15 per meal
  • Chinatown: Dim sum $10-18 per person
  • Food trucks: $10-15 per meal
  • Grocery stores: Trader Joe's, Safeway $6-10 meals
  • vs. Sit-down restaurants: $40-60 per person after tax + tip

🚗 Transportation

Public Transit Works (But is Slow to Stadium)

SF has decent public transit within the city, but getting to Levi's Stadium is complicated:

  • BART: Bay Area Rapid Transit doesn't go to stadium
  • Caltrain + VTA: SF → San Jose (1 hour) + light rail (30 min) = 90+ min total
  • Driving: 1 hour without traffic, 90+ min with traffic
  • Recommendation: Stay near San Jose if attending match, use public transit for SF sightseeing

Within San Francisco

  • BART: Rapid transit, $3-10 per trip
  • Muni (buses/streetcars): $3 per trip
  • Cable cars: $8 per ride (tourist experience, not practical transport)
  • Clipper Card: Reloadable transit card for all systems

Rental Car Costs

  • Rental: $60-100/day
  • Insurance: $15-30/day
  • Gas: $4.50-5.50/gallon (highest in US)
  • Hotel parking: $40-70/night in SF
  • Stadium parking: $75-175 per game
  • Bridge tolls: $6-8 each way (Golden Gate, Bay Bridge)

🌡️ Weather in June/July

⚠️ "Coldest Winter I Ever Spent Was Summer in San Francisco"

San Francisco summer is NOT warm:

  • Temperature: 55-70°F (13-21°C) - surprisingly cool!
  • Fog: Very common, especially mornings and evenings
  • Wind: Can be strong and cold
  • What to pack: Layers! Jacket/sweater essential even in summer
  • Santa Clara (stadium): Warmer and sunnier (75-85°F / 24-29°C)

Many international visitors are shocked by how cool SF is in summer. The Bay Area has microclimates - SF is foggy/cool, but drive 30 miles inland and it's hot.

🛡️ Safety

Generally Safe Tourist Areas

  • Fisherman's Wharf (touristy, safe)
  • Union Square (shopping district)
  • North Beach (Italian district)
  • Marina District
  • Chinatown (daytime)

Be Cautious / Avoid

  • Tenderloin: High drug activity, avoid especially at night
  • Parts of SoMa: Variable safety, research specific blocks
  • Bayview/Hunter's Point: High crime
  • Car break-ins: EXTREMELY common - never leave ANYTHING visible in car

⚠️ SF Has Highest Car Break-In Rate in US

San Francisco has epidemic levels of car break-ins. NEVER leave anything visible in your car, even for 5 minutes. Thieves break windows for bags, phones, even sunglasses. Rental cars are prime targets.
