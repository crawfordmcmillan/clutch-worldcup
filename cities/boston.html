⚾ Boston, Massachusetts

Complete World Cup Visitor's Guide

⚠️ CRITICAL: "Boston" Games Are NOT in Boston

Gillette Stadium is in Foxborough, Massachusetts - 28 miles (45 km) southwest of downtown Boston.

It's actually closer to Providence, Rhode Island (22 miles) than to Boston! Many international visitors book Boston hotels assuming the stadium is nearby, then discover on match day they need a 90-minute journey each way.

❌ Hotel Tax Rebates DO NOT EXIST

If you're from Europe, Australia, or Canada, you're used to VAT refunds. The US has NO hotel tax refunds for international visitors. That 14.75% is gone forever.

2. Restaurant & Tipping Costs

What menu shows: $20 burger

What you actually pay:

  • Menu price: $20.00
  • Sales tax (6.25%): +$1.25
  • Tip (18-22% on pre-tax): +$3.60-4.40
  • Total: $24.85-25.65 (24-28% more than menu)

Tipping is MANDATORY in Boston

Unlike Europe where 5-10% is optional, US servers earn $2.13/hour base wage. They depend on tips. 18-22% is expected minimum for adequate service.

  • Restaurants: 18-22% of pre-tax bill
  • Bars: $1-2 per drink or 20% of tab
  • Hotel housekeeping: $3-5 per night (leave daily)
  • Uber/taxi: 15-20% of fare
  • Coffee shops: $1 per drink (or 15-20% if digital screen)

⚖️ Massachusetts State Laws (What's Different Here)

✅ Cannabis LEGAL

Recreational use legal since 2016

  • Adults 21+ can possess up to 1 oz
  • Licensed dispensaries exist
  • Use only in private, NOT public
  • ⚠️ Still illegal on federal property!

🔫 Firearms Restricted

License to Carry required

  • Open carry requires license
  • Police chiefs have discretion
  • NOT like Texas/Missouri
  • Very strict compared to other states

🍺 Alcohol Laws

Relatively restrictive

  • 21+ to purchase (universal)
  • Limited Sunday sales hours
  • Public consumption illegal
  • Open containers in cars illegal

🚗 Traffic & Driving

Unique rules for Massachusetts

  • Speed limit: 65 mph on highways
  • Right on red: ALLOWED but...
  • ⚠️ 90% of signals have "No Turn on Red"
  • DUI: 0.08% (serious penalties)

⚠️ Right Turn on Red Confusion

Massachusetts was the LAST state to allow right turns on red (1980). City officials immediately added "No Turn on Red" signs to 90% of intersections. Always check for signs before turning. Boston area especially restrictive.

🍽️ Food & Dining Culture

Boston-Specific Notes

  • Seafood: Famous for lobster, clam chowder, oysters (but expensive $25-50/person)
  • Portions: 42% larger than European equivalents (one meal = 2-3 servings)
  • Tipping: 18-22% mandatory (servers depend on tips)
  • Water: Free tap water at restaurants (always ask)
  • Doggy bags: Taking leftovers home is normal, not rude

Payment Process (Different from Europe)

  1. Server brings bill to table
  2. You give credit card
  3. Server takes card away, processes payment
  4. Returns with receipt showing total + blank tip line
  5. You write tip amount and new total
  6. Server processes tip later

⚠️ Don't leave cash on table and walk out - wait for server to acknowledge

🚇 Getting Around Boston

Public Transit (MBTA - "The T")

Good for: Downtown Boston, Cambridge, major attractions

NOT good for: Gillette Stadium, suburbs, late night

  • Subway lines: Red, Orange, Blue, Green (easy to navigate)
  • Hours: ~5:30am-12:30am (varies by line)
  • Cost: $2.40 per ride (CharlieCard), $2.90 (paper ticket)
  • 7-day pass: $25 unlimited
  • Coverage: Downtown only, NOT comprehensive like Europe

Uber/Lyft (Rideshare)

  • Normal pricing: $15-25 within Boston
  • Surge pricing: 1.5-3X during peak times
  • Match days: Expect 2-3X surge to/from Gillette
  • Tipping: 15-20% expected

Rental Car

Necessary if: Staying near stadium, visiting suburbs, multi-city trip

Costs:

  • Rental: $50-100/day (compact to mid-size)
  • Insurance: $15-30/day (check if credit card covers)
  • Gas: $3.50-4.50/gallon
  • Parking: $20-50/day in Boston, free in suburbs

⚠️ Left-Hand Drive Countries

If you're from UK, Ireland, Japan, Australia, or other left-hand drive countries: American cars drive on the RIGHT side of the road. This takes significant adjustment. Consider public transit in Boston if uncomfortable driving.
